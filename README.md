# Privacy Policy for Daily Motivation: Inspire

**Last updated: August 14, 2026**

Daily Motivation: Inspire respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

Most information created or saved in Daily Motivation: Inspire is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

- Journal entries
- Mood entries
- Favorite quotes
- Streak and activity information
- Achievements and progress
- App settings
- Profile information you choose to enter
- Daily goals and motivation preferences

This information is not transmitted to us as part of the normal use of these features.

## 2. Translation Feature

Daily Motivation: Inspire includes a translation feature.

When translation is required, the app sends:

- The quote text being translated
- The selected target language

This information is sent to our translation service so that the translated quote can be returned to the app.

Translation requests may be processed through third-party infrastructure and artificial intelligence services, including Render and OpenAI.

The translation information is used only to provide the translation functionality.

We do not use translation requests for advertising or user tracking.

## 3. Personal Identification

Translation requests are not intentionally linked to your name, email address, account, or other personal identity by Daily Motivation: Inspire.

The app does not require you to create an online account to use its main features.

## 4. Advertising and Tracking

Daily Motivation: Inspire does not currently use personal information for:

- Targeted advertising
- Advertising measurement
- Cross-app tracking
- Selling personal information to data brokers

## 5. Third-Party Services

Some functionality may rely on third-party service providers.

These may include:

- Render, for backend infrastructure
- OpenAI, for translation processing

These providers may process information necessary to provide their services and may handle information according to their own privacy and security policies.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information transmitted outside the device and use secure network connections when communicating with online services.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Daily Motivation: Inspire is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe personal information has been provided to us improperly, please contact us.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or when new features are introduced.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
